Where the pieces come from

Each piece is made by a message on the system's control channel. A channel grant (a radio keyed up) makes a T, O or I. An affiliation (a radio joined a talkgroup) makes a J or L. A registration or deregistration (a radio came onto the system or left it) makes an S or Z.

Only one message in many makes a piece: the first to come after the last piece appears, of a kind whose piece is still in the bag of seven. So each piece comes as often as any other, and the game goes at its own pace. A system that never sends a kind gets its pieces from the others after a few seconds.
